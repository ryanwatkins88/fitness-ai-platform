# Muscle Guard Protocol — Viral Questions & Reel Hooks

Content bank for Facebook Reels / IG Reels / TikTok. Angle: GLP-1 users (Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) are losing weight but also losing muscle — showing up as "Ozempic face" (gaunt, hollow, aged look) and a soft/deflated "skinny fat" body instead of a toned one. Muscle Guard is positioned as the protocol that fixes the missing piece: protecting muscle *while* the weight comes off.

**Disclaimer to include somewhere in your content/bio:** This is general fitness/nutrition education, not medical advice. Anyone on a GLP-1 medication should talk to their prescribing doctor about diet and exercise changes.

---

## How to use this doc

- **On-screen text hook** (Section 1) = the first 1-2 seconds of the Reel, big bold text over B-roll or you talking.
- **Caption question** (Section 2, the 100) = drives comments/shares. Post it as the caption, or say it out loud as the spoken hook, then pivot to "...that's exactly why I built the Muscle Guard protocol."
- Rotate categories so you're not posting 10 "myth-busting" videos in a row — mix a stat hook, a self-diagnostic hook, and a hot take across a week.
- Every video should end on some version of: *"If you want the plan that protects your muscle while you lose the weight, that's what Muscle Guard is."*

## Section 1: Research-Backed Hook Lines (for on-screen text / voiceover)

These are the "stat drop" lines — punchy, quotable, and grounded in real research so they hold up if someone fact-checks you in the comments.

| Hook line (say/show this) | What it's based on |
|---|---|
| "Up to 40% of the weight you lose on a GLP-1 can be muscle — not fat." | Clinical trial body-composition substudies (STEP/SURMOUNT) show lean tissue making up roughly 25–45% of total weight lost. |
| "Semaglutide users lost nearly double the lean mass of tirzepatide users in trial data." | STEP 1 substudy (~45% lean mass of total loss, semaglutide) vs. SURMOUNT-1 substudy (~25%, tirzepatide). |
| "It's not the drug that costs you muscle — it's the missing plan." | Researchers/clinicians attribute muscle loss to calorie deficit + low protein + no resistance training, not a direct drug effect. |
| "Resistance training plus enough protein can cut GLP-1 muscle loss to almost zero." | Consistent finding across GLP-1 muscle-preservation research and clinical guidance. |
| "Ozempic face isn't really about your face — it's about muscle loss showing up first where you have the least fat to spare." | Clinical descriptions of "Ozempic face" as a lean-tissue/fat loss effect from rapid weight loss. |
| "That '3kg of lean mass lost' headline? Only a fraction of it is actual muscle — but that fraction is the part you can't afford to lose." | Lean mass measurements include glycogen, water, and inflammation fluid, not just contractile muscle — but real muscle loss still occurs. |
| "2-3 strength sessions a week is the number researchers point to for protecting muscle on a GLP-1." | General clinical/expert guidance for muscle preservation during GLP-1-driven weight loss. |
| "You can lose the fat and keep your shape. Most people just aren't told how." | Positioning line — pairs with any of the above. |
| "Muscle doesn't come back as easily as it leaves." | Standard exercise physiology point on muscle protein turnover — use as a soft-urgency line, not a scare tactic. |

## Suggested posting rhythm

- **3-4x/week:** rotate one from each category so the feed doesn't feel repetitive.
- Lead with **Section 1 hook lines** as text-on-screen; use the matching category from Section 2 as the caption or spoken question.
- Save Category **I (direct CTA)** for roughly 1 in 5 posts — too many direct pitches in a row kills reach; let the education/self-diagnostic/hot-take posts build the case first.
