# FitPro Leads — 30-Day Content Calendar

**Goal:** Attract fitness coaches, trainers, and gym/studio owners who want more
leads and better conversion, and get them to apply for the free case-study
spots (limited run, transitioning to a paid offer after).

**Offer being marketed:** Done-for-you content + lead tracking + client
conversion confirmation, run as a live case study for a small group of coaches,
free for a limited time, then paid.

**Target avatar:** Independent coach / PT / online fitness coach / small
studio or gym owner who is already posting (or trying to) but has no system
turning followers into booked calls into paying clients. They feel the
"post and pray" cycle and are tired of inconsistent income.

---

## How to use this calendar

Each day gives you **one core idea** with:
- A **spoken hook** (say this in the first 1–2 seconds of a Reel/TikTok, and
  put it as on-screen text too)
- A **written hook** (first line for IG feed, Threads, Facebook, carousel slide 1)
- A **core caption** (built for IG / Facebook / Threads — these three share a
  caption style)
- A **LinkedIn reframe** (one line to shift tone from "social" to "B2B insight")
- A **CTA** and any platform notes

Post the same core idea everywhere on the same day, adapted per the notes
below. Don't rewrite five completely different posts a day — reuse the core
idea, change the wrapper.

### Platform cheat sheet

| Platform | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| TikTok / IG Reels | Talking-head or on-screen text video, 20–45 sec | Hook must land in first 2 seconds, on-screen captions on |
| Instagram feed/carousel | Single image quote or 4–6 slide carousel | Slide 1 = the hook, verbatim |
| Threads | Text-only post | Shortest version of the hook, written to invite replies, no hashtags |
| Facebook | Native text post, ideally in coach/gym-owner groups (with permission) | Longest allowed caption, more storytelling, less hashtag-dependent |
| LinkedIn | Text post, first-person authority voice | Reframe as a business insight for "coaches/gym owners," 3–5 hashtags max, no emoji-heavy hook |

### CTA bank (rotate these)

- "Comment **LEADS** and I'll send you the application."
- "5 spots. Apply from the link in bio."
- "DM me **CASE STUDY** and I'll walk you through it."
- "Tag a coach who's stuck in the post-and-pray cycle."
- "Save this — you'll want it when you sit down to plan content."

### Hashtag bank (IG / TikTok only — skip on Threads, light on LinkedIn)

`#fitnesscoach #onlinecoaching #personaltrainer #coachlife #fitnessbusiness #gymowner #clientacquisition #coachingbusiness #fitnessmarketing #leadgeneration`

### Guardrail — read before posting

This is a **real, unproven-yet case study**. Do not invent numbers, results,
or testimonials that don't exist ("got a client 12 leads in 3 days," fake
quotes from "coaches in the program," etc.). Every post below is written to
be true on day one: pain points, process, and a transparent offer. Once real
results exist, swap the "case study update" days for actual numbers and
(permissioned) quotes — don't fill them in early.

## Week 1 — Naming the problem

### Day 1 — The real problem isn't content
- **Spoken hook:** "You don't have a content problem. You have a lead-tracking problem."
- **Written hook:** You don't have a content problem. You have a lead-tracking problem.
- **Caption:** Most coaches think if they just posted more, clients would show up. So they post. And post. And still get the same three likes from other coaches. Here's the truth: content brings attention. It doesn't automatically bring clients. Without a system to catch who's interested, follow up, and confirm who actually becomes a paying client, all that content is just noise. That's the gap FitPro Leads exists to close.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Open with: "Most fitness coaches don't have a marketing problem — they have a pipeline problem." Then the same explanation, closing with an invitation to a free case study instead of a hard sell.
- **CTA:** "Comment LEADS and I'll send you the application."

### Day 2 — Confession
- **Spoken hook:** "I used to post every single day and get zero clients from it."
- **Written hook:** I used to post daily and get zero clients from it. Here's what changed.
- **Caption:** For months I treated posting like the goal instead of the start of a process. Likes went up. Bookings didn't. What actually moved the needle: knowing exactly who engaged, following up like it was my job (because it was), and tracking who turned into a real, paying client — not just a follow. That's the system I'm now building for other coaches, for free, as a case study.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Frame as a lesson learned in building a service business — "vanity metrics vs. pipeline metrics."
- **CTA:** "Save this if you've ever felt the same."

### Day 3 — Myth-busting
- **Spoken hook:** "More followers will not get you more clients. I'll prove it."
- **Written hook:** More followers ≠ more clients. Here's the math nobody talks about.
- **Caption:** A coach with 2,000 followers and a real follow-up system will out-earn a coach with 20,000 followers and no process, every time. Followers are an audience. Clients come from what happens after someone raises their hand — the DM, the comment, the "tell me more." If nothing catches that moment, it doesn't matter how big the audience is.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** "Audience size is a vanity metric. Conversion rate is a revenue metric." Same content, more direct B2B framing.
- **CTA:** "Tag a coach who needs to hear this."

### Day 4 — The 3-step framework
- **Spoken hook:** "There are only three steps between a stranger and a paying client. Most coaches are missing two of them."
- **Written hook:** Stranger → client only takes 3 steps. Most coaches are only doing step one.
- **Caption:** Step 1: content that stops the scroll and speaks to a real pain point. Step 2: a way to track who actually engages — not just likes, but comments and DMs that signal real interest. Step 3: a follow-up process that turns "I'm interested" into "I'm booked." Most coaches only do step 1, then wonder why nothing converts.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as "the 3-stage funnel every coaching business needs" — attract, track, convert.
- **CTA:** "Which step are you missing? Comment below."

### Day 5 — Case study announcement
- **Spoken hook:** "I'm doing something a little different this month — for free."
- **Written hook:** Starting this month, I'm running a free case study for 5 coaches. Here's what it is.
- **Caption:** I'm taking on 5 fitness coaches and building their content, tracking their leads, and confirming who converts to paying clients — for free, as a case study. Why free? Because I want to prove this works in the open before I ever charge for it. No fine print, no upsell mid-way. After the case study wraps, this becomes a paid service. If you want in while it's free, this is the moment.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as launching a "pilot program" — professional framing of the same offer, emphasize transparency.
- **CTA:** "Comment LEADS and I'll send you the application."

### Day 6 — Objection: what does "done for you" mean
- **Spoken hook:** "When I say 'done for you,' here's exactly what I mean."
- **Written hook:** "Done-for-you" gets thrown around a lot. Here's exactly what it means in this case study.
- **Caption:** It means: I write your hooks and captions. I track who engages and who reaches out. I confirm, with you, who actually becomes a paying client — not just a lead in a spreadsheet. You keep showing up as the coach. I handle the content and the tracking behind it. That's the whole deal.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Bullet-point the scope cleanly — professional audiences respond well to a clear deliverables list.
- **CTA:** "Questions about scope? Drop them below."

### Day 7 — Weekend engagement
- **Spoken hook:** "What's actually stopping you from posting consistently? Be honest."
- **Written hook:** Real talk — what's stopping you from posting consistently?
- **Caption:** Not enough time? Don't know what to say? Post it and nothing happens so why bother? Drop your honest answer below — I read every one, and it's exactly the stuff I'm building FitPro Leads to solve.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Turn into a light poll-style post: "Coaches — what's your biggest blocker: time, ideas, or follow-through on leads?"
- **CTA:** "Comment your honest answer."

## Week 2 — Teaching the system (give real value away)

### Day 8 — 3 hook types
- **Spoken hook:** "Here are the 3 hooks that stop coaches from scrolling past your post."
- **Written hook:** 3 hook types that stop coaches mid-scroll (steal these).
- **Caption:** 1) The confession — "I used to think X, until Y." 2) The myth-bust — "Everyone tells you to do X. Here's why that's wrong." 3) The blunt question — "What's actually stopping you from X?" Rotate these three and you'll never stare at a blank caption box again.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as a "content framework" — coaches building a business will save this.
- **CTA:** "Save this post for your next content session."

### Day 9 — Lead vs. prospect vs. client
- **Spoken hook:** "A lead, a prospect, and a client are not the same thing. Confusing them is costing you money."
- **Written hook:** Lead ≠ prospect ≠ client. Here's the difference, and why it matters.
- **Caption:** A **lead** is anyone who raises a hand — a comment, a follow, a DM. A **prospect** is a lead you've actually talked to about their goals. A **client** is someone paying you. Most coaches track none of these separately, so they have no idea where people are actually falling out of the process. Fix the tracking, fix the leaks.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as basic sales-funnel literacy for coaches — many haven't been taught this.
- **CTA:** "Which stage do you lose the most people at? Comment below."

### Day 10 — Metrics that actually matter
- **Spoken hook:** "Stop checking your like count. Check this instead."
- **Written hook:** Likes don't pay your bills. Track these 3 numbers instead.
- **Caption:** 1) Comments/DMs per post (real interest signals). 2) Reply rate — how many people you actually follow up with. 3) Lead-to-client rate — how many of those conversations turn into a paying client. These three numbers tell you if your content is working. Likes tell you nothing.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as "vanity metrics vs. pipeline metrics coaches should track weekly."
- **CTA:** "Which of these three are you not tracking yet?"

### Day 11 — The DM script
- **Spoken hook:** "Here's exactly what to say when someone comments on your post."
- **Written hook:** Someone comments on your post. Now what? Here's the script.
- **Caption:** Don't just reply with a heart. Reply, then follow up in DMs with something like: "Hey [name], saw your comment — curious, is [pain point] something you're dealing with right now?" That one question turns a comment into a real conversation, and a real conversation is where clients actually come from.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as "the follow-up habit most coaches skip" — professional tone, same script.
- **CTA:** "Try this on your next comment and tell me how it goes."

### Day 12 — 4 content pillars
- **Spoken hook:** "Every coach needs exactly 4 types of content. Here they are."
- **Written hook:** 4 content pillars every coach needs (and nothing more).
- **Caption:** 1) Pain point — what your ideal client is struggling with. 2) Myth-bust — a belief that's holding them back. 3) Proof/process — what you actually do and why it works. 4) Invitation — a clear, low-pressure way to start working with you. Rotate these four and content planning stops being the hard part.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as a "content pillar framework" — clean, bulleted, save-worthy for a business audience.
- **CTA:** "Save this before you plan next week's content."

### Day 13 — Consistency isn't the whole answer
- **Spoken hook:** "'Just be consistent' is the most incomplete advice in fitness marketing."
- **Written hook:** "Just post consistently" is true — and also missing half the story.
- **Caption:** Consistency gets you an audience. It doesn't automatically get you clients. You can post daily for a year and still have no system for turning attention into paying work. Consistency is necessary. It's not sufficient. Pair it with tracking and follow-up, or you're just building an audience that never buys.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Present as a contrarian but constructive take — good for engagement in a professional feed.
- **CTA:** "Agree or disagree? Comment below."

### Day 14 — Weekend engagement
- **Spoken hook:** "Be honest — what's your biggest content struggle? Pick one."
- **Written hook:** Poll: what's your #1 content struggle right now?
- **Caption:** A) Coming up with ideas B) Actually posting consistently C) Getting engagement D) Turning engagement into clients. Comment the letter — I'll reply to every single one with something you can try this week.
- **LinkedIn reframe:** Same poll structure, framed as market research for coaches — invites genuine responses.
- **CTA:** "Comment your letter (A, B, C, or D)."
