# Engagement Questions — Small Business Owners & Entrepreneurs Group

Built for a "learn, connect, grow" community group. Two goals at once:
get people participating, and surface real answers that tell you who
actually needs the AI business assessment — without the group feeling
mined for leads.

## How to use these

- Post 2-3x a week, not daily — a group needs room to breathe between
  prompts.
- Mix light/connection questions with diagnostic ones roughly 2:1. If
  every post is secretly about pain points, people feel it.
- **Never pitch in the thread.** If someone's answer reveals a real
  pain point (marked 🎯 below), follow up with them 1:1 in DM later —
  same principle as the comment-to-DM approach in the Reels content.
  The group post builds the relationship; the DM does the actual
  conversation.
- Answer your own question first, honestly, before anyone else does —
  groups engage more when the host goes first.

---

## Connection / icebreaker (pure engagement, builds trust)

1. Where are you located, and what's the business you're building?
2. What's one win you had this week — doesn't have to be big?
3. What's a book, podcast, or person that changed how you think about
   business?
4. Tag someone in this group who's helped you out this year, even in a
   small way.
5. What's the most "entrepreneur" thing that's happened to you this
   month? (Good or chaotic, either counts.)
6. Coffee or no coffee before you start your workday? (Just for fun —
   these matter for keeping the group feeling human.)

---

## Content & marketing 🎯

7. Be honest — how often do you actually post content for your
   business versus how often you tell yourself you "should"?
8. What's harder for you: coming up with content ideas, or actually
   posting consistently?
9. Have you ever posted something you were proud of and gotten...
   nothing back? What happened next?
10. Do you actually know which piece of content brought you your last
    client or customer? Or is it a guess?

*(These surface people whose real gap is content/attention — a good
fit for the content side of the assessment.)*

---

## Leads & follow-up 🎯

11. When someone comments or DMs asking about your business, what's
    your actual next step? Be specific.
12. Raise your hand (🙋) if you've ever lost track of a lead who
    reached out and you never circled back.
13. How do you currently know whether a lead turned into a paying
    customer — or do you not really track that?

*(These are the strongest signal for the follow-up/tracking gap the
assessment is built to catch.)*

---

## Automation & systems 🎯

14. What's one repetitive task in your business you wish just ran on
    autopilot?
15. Do you have any actual automations set up right now? What are
    they, if any?
16. If you could clone yourself for one task this week, what would it
    be?

*(Strong signal for the "established business, needs automation quick
wins" branch of the assessment.)*

---

## Direction & planning 🎯

17. Honestly — do you have an actual plan for the next 90 days, or are
    you winging it?
18. What's the one thing you wish someone would just tell you to focus
    on right now?
19. Newer business owners — what's the biggest thing you're unsure
    about at this stage?

*(Strong signal for the "newer business, needs a starter plan" branch
of the assessment.)*

---

## Growth / bottleneck (general, high-signal)

20. What does "success" look like for your business this year, in one
    sentence?
21. What's the single biggest bottleneck between you and your next
    level right now?
22. If you got one free hour of expert help on your business this
    week, what would you actually spend it on?

*(#21 and #22 are close to a direct pitch without being one — great
ones to watch closely for DM follow-ups.)*

---

## Notes

- Don't turn every 🎯 answer into an immediate DM — read the room.
  Someone who answers once in passing is different from someone who
  clearly keeps circling the same frustration across multiple posts.
- If a thread gets several similar 🎯 answers, that's a signal for
  which pain point to lead with in your next Reels/Facebook post too —
  real, current language from real people beats a guessed hook every
  time.
- Keep a simple running note (even just a notes app) of who answered
  which 🎯 questions and what they said — that's your actual warm list
  for outreach, built without ever having pitched in the group.
